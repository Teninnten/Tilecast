# Tilecast

Show your iPad's screen on your Windows PC, over your own Wi-Fi.

Tilecast uses the **Screen Mirroring** that's already on your iPad (or iPhone). There's nothing to install on the iPad.

## Download

**[Download Tilecast for Windows](https://github.com/Teninnten/Tilecast/releases/latest/download/Tilecast-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

Or see every version under [Releases](https://github.com/Teninnten/Tilecast/releases).

## Install

1. Run `Tilecast-Setup.exe`.
2. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Tilecast is a small free app without a paid signing certificate.
3. Click **Yes** when Windows asks to allow changes. The installer lets the iPad through Windows Firewall.
4. Click **Next**, **Install** and **Finish**. Tilecast opens by itself.

## Mirror your iPad

1. Put the iPad and the PC on the same Wi-Fi.
2. On the iPad, swipe down from the top-right corner and tap **Screen Mirroring**.
3. Pick the name Tilecast shows in big letters.

While mirroring: **F** full screen, **T** keep on top, **S** screenshot, **M** mute.

## iPad doesn't list the PC?

- Tilecast shows a red bar with a button that fixes the usual causes.
- The PC's Wi-Fi must be **Private**: Windows Settings › Network & internet › Wi-Fi › your network › Network profile type › Private.
- School, office and café Wi-Fi often hide devices from each other. A phone hotspot usually works.

## Privacy

Your screen only travels over your own Wi-Fi. Once a day Tilecast asks GitHub whether a new version is out; that's the only thing it does online, and Settings › General turns it off.
