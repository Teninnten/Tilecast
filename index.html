Mirror your iPad to your PC.

Tilecast puts your iPad's screen in a window on your Windows PC, over your own Wi-Fi. It uses the Screen Mirroring already on your iPad or iPhone, so there's nothing to install there.

Same Wi-Fi

Put the iPad and the PC on the same Wi-Fi network.

Screen Mirroring

On the iPad, swipe down from the top-right corner and tap Screen Mirroring.

3

Choose the name Tilecast shows in big letters. Your screen appears on the PC.

Features

Keys while mirroring
F

Full screen

The iPad fills the monitor. Esc to leave.

Z

Fill

Zooms past the black bars around widescreen videos.

T

On top

Stays above other windows, handy for following along.

S

Snap

Saves a screenshot to Pictures › Tilecast.

M

Mute

iPad sound plays on the PC, with its own volume.

PIN

Code for new iPads

On shared Wi-Fi, each new iPad types a 4-digit code once.

✓

Fixes itself

Finds the usual reasons an iPad can't see the PC, with a button to fix them.

●

Record

Optionally saves each session as a video. Off unless you turn it on.

Install

Takes a minute

  1. Run Tilecast-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Tilecast is a small free app without a paid signing certificate.
  3. Click Yes when Windows asks: the installer lets the iPad through Windows Firewall.
Privacy

Stays on your Wi-Fi

Your screen goes straight from the iPad to the PC, never through the internet. Once a day Tilecast asks GitHub whether a new version is out, and that's all it does online. You can switch that off in Settings.
